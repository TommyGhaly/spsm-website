To offer compassionate care, support, and fellowship to the elderly and those who are sick, ensuring they feel valued, connected, and spiritually uplifted within our church community.

We establish regular visitation schedules, provide assistance with practical needs, facilitate prayer and spiritual encouragement, and create programs that address the unique challenges faced by the elderly and the sick — fostering a strong sense of belonging and care.
