Fr. Armia was ordained a priest by His Grace Bishop Timotheos on November 27, 2015, serving in Anba Karas and St. Philopater Coptic Orthodox Church in Zagazig, Egypt. In January 2024, His Grace Bishop David appointed Fr. Armia as the priest for St. Philopater Mercurius & St. Mina Coptic Orthodox Church in Wayland, MA.
