Fr. Youhanna was ordained a priest by His Holiness the late Pope Shenouda III on January 12, 2002, and served in St. Mark Coptic Orthodox Church in Natick, Massachusetts. In 2016, His Grace Bishop David set Fr. Youhanna as priest for St. Philopater Mercurius & St. Mina Coptic Orthodox Church in Wayland, Massachusetts. In November 2022, His Grace Bishop David elevated Fr. Youhanna to the rank of hegumen.
